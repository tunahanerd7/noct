Terms of Use

Last updated: November 2026

1. Who can use Noct

You must be at least 17 years old to use Noct. Laws about alcohol and venue entry still apply to you; Noct does not change them.

2. Your account

You are responsible for your account and for keeping your password safe. Give accurate information, including your birth date. One person, one account.

3. Zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive users

You may not post or send content that is sexual, violent, hateful, harassing, threatening, illegal, spam or that impersonates someone. You may not bully, stalk or abuse other users. Users can report and block anyone. We review reports and may remove content and suspend or delete accounts that break these rules, without notice.

4. Your content

You keep ownership of the photos and text you post. You give Noct permission to store and show them inside the app as needed to run the service. Moments you share at a venue are visible to everyone for 24 hours. Don't post content you don't have the right to share, and don't post photos of people who don't want to be shown.

5. Location features

Check-in only happens when you confirm it, and only near the venue. Your friends can see the venue you're checked in at unless you hide it in Privacy settings. The radar shows other users an approximate location (about 100 meters) only while you have it open. "Safe night" shares your location only with the friends you pick, until you end it.

6. Meeting people

Noct helps you find venues and people nearby. Use good judgment, meet in public places and look out for each other. In an emergency, call 112. Noct is not an emergency service and is not responsible for the conduct of users or venues.

7. Venues and artists

Venue information, events, perks and announcements are provided by the people who manage each venue and may change. Check with the venue directly. To manage a venue you must actually work there; to have an artist profile you must be that artist or creator. We review every request and may ask you to verify. Venue teams may only use guests' reservation details to handle that reservation.

8. Ending your account

You can delete your account at any time in Settings. We may suspend accounts that break these terms.

9. Changes

We may update these terms. If changes are important, we'll let you know in the app.
