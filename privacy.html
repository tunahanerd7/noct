Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

What we collect

  • Account: email, username, birth date, gender. Your email and birth date are private and not shown to other users.
  • Personal info (optional): your name and phone number, if you add them or enter them for a reservation or venue request. Only you can see them in your profile.
  • Profile: photos, bio and interests you add.
  • Location: only while the app is open. Used to show nearby venues, check you in, and (if you open the radar) to show nearby users an approximate location (about 100 m).
  • Activity: friends, plans, check-ins, venues visited, online status, messages and photos you send, reports and blocks.

How we use it

To run Noct's features: the map, radar, check-ins, friends, chat and plans; to keep the community safe; and to fix problems.

Who can see what

  • Everyone signed in: your username, profile photo, bio, interests, photos and badges.
  • Your friends: your online status, last seen, the venue you're at and venues you've visited (you can hide online status and venue in Settings).
  • A venue you send a reservation request to: the name and phone number you enter in that request, only for that booking. Everyone on that venue's team (owner, managers, event managers and door staff) can see it.
  • If you ask to manage a venue: the Noct team sees the name, phone number and note you enter, to verify you. Your username and role are shown to the rest of that venue's team.
  • People you block can't see you on the radar or message you.
  • We don't sell your data and we don't show ads.

Where it's stored

Data is stored with Google Firebase (Google Cloud).

Deleting your data

Delete your account in Settings → Delete Account. This removes your profile, personal info, photos, moments, plans, likes, matches, reservations and venue requests, and removes you from any venue team. Messages you sent may remain visible to the person you sent them to.

Safety

Reports go to the Noct team. Accounts that break our rules can be suspended.

Children

Noct is not for anyone under 17.
