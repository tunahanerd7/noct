This draft describes how personal data is processed in the Noct app. For users in Türkiye, it is intended to support the information notice required under Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (“KVKK”). This notice provides information; it does not replace any separate consent that may be legally required.

2. Personal data we process

  • Account and identity: username, email address, date of birth, optional gender information, profile photo, account creation and sign-in information.
  • Contact details and contact matching: optional phone number and full name; if you enable friend discovery, contact names and phone numbers/email addresses stored in your device’s address book. For matching, the app normalizes and hashes contact values on the device instead of uploading the raw address book as a list. The hash lookups and matching Noct account identifiers may be processed by our service providers.
  • Profile and preferences: bio, music preferences, interests, selected areas, follows, badges, privacy choices and profile photos.
  • Location and nightlife activity: device location to show nearby places; approximate location for radar when you enable it; venue and check-in information when you confirm a check-in; and location, venue name and update time in Safe Night sharing with friends you select. Safe Night location updates only while Noct is open. We may also process venue visit history, event attendance/interest, plans, votes, venue ratings and perk use.
  • User content and communications: photos and moments, captions, chat messages and photos, plan notes, event/venue/artist content, notification preferences, reports and block records.
  • Reservations and applications: name, phone number, party size, date and note you submit for a reservation; information and verification materials submitted in venue/artist applications and event proposals.
  • Technical and usage data: app version, device and operating system information, push notification token, connection and crash/error records. Technical logs needed for security, abuse prevention and troubleshooting may also be generated.

3. Purposes and legal grounds

We process data to create accounts and authenticate users; provide the map, event discovery, friends, messaging, plans, check-ins, reservations, venue/artist pages and notifications; make personalized suggestions; host user content; respond to support requests; protect the service and prevent fraud or abuse; and understand service performance and fix errors.

The applicable legal ground must be determined for each data category and purpose. Grounds that may apply include performance of a contract, compliance with a legal obligation, establishment/exercise/defence of a right, or legitimate interests where those interests do not override your fundamental rights. Location, contacts, photos and notifications depend on your choice to use the relevant feature and the permissions you grant through your device. Where consent is legally required, it should be collected separately from this notice and through a freely given choice.

4. Who may access data and who may receive it?

  • Other users: your public profile information; and friendship/event status or check-in information according to your privacy choices and the visibility rules in the app. Moments you share at a venue may be visible for 24 hours. Radar and Safe Night location are shared only when you enable the relevant feature and with the people described for that feature.
  • Venues: a venue receiving your reservation request can see the name and phone number you entered for that request. When you use a perk, the venue may see your username for that transaction. Venue teams may access reservation details to respond to requests. Other event/check-in statistics are intended to be shown as aggregated counts where practicable.
  • Authorized Noct personnel: limited access for support, application verification, report review and security operations.
  • Service providers: Google Firebase/Google Cloud and Cloudflare for hosting, authentication and infrastructure; Apple Push Notification service and Firebase Cloud Messaging for notifications; and crash-reporting/diagnostic services. Apple or Google sign-in providers are involved if you choose those sign-in methods.
  • Maps and open-data services: Apple MapKit and OpenStreetMap/Wikimedia services may be used to provide nearby places and map content. Your account or name is not intentionally included in map-area requests; service providers may process IP addresses and request/device data to deliver their services under their own policies.
  • Public authorities: only where required by applicable law or necessary and legally permitted to protect rights.

We do not sell personal data. Noct currently does not display third-party targeted advertising inside the app. Promotion of Noct on other platforms may be subject to those platforms’ own data practices.

6. Location, contacts and notification permissions

You can use some parts of Noct without granting location permission, but nearby venue and other location-based features may not work. Radar is optional and shares approximate location while enabled. You start Safe Night, select the friends who can receive it and can end sharing; location updates only while the app is open. Address book access is optional, and you can turn off the “findable by contacts” choice in the app. You can change notification permission in your device settings at any time.

7. Retention

We retain personal data for as long as needed for the relevant purpose and subject to applicable legal retention requirements. You can delete your account in the app. Deletion is initiated to erase or de-identify data linked to your account; limited records may be retained where required by law, to resolve disputes, for security records, or to preserve the integrity of communications with other users. Messages you sent may remain visible in recipients’ chats. Anonymous or aggregated venue statistics may remain.
