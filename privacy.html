Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

What we collect

  • Account: email, username, birth date, gender. Your email and birth date are private and not shown to other users.
  • Personal info (optional): your name and phone number, if you add them or enter them for a reservation or venue request. Only you can see them in your profile.
  • Profile: photos, bio, music taste and the areas you pick.
  • Location: only while the app is open. Used to show nearby venues, to ask whether you're at a venue (check-in), for the radar if you open it, and for "Safe night" if you start it.
  • Activity: friends, follows, plans and votes, check-ins, events you're going to, venue ratings, moments, messages and photos you send, perks you use, reports and blocks.

How we use it

To run Noct's features (Tonight, the map, check-ins, friends, chat, plans, venue and artist pages), to suggest nights you might like, to keep the community safe, and to fix problems. Reminders are scheduled on your phone; we don't send marketing messages.

Who can see what

  • Everyone signed in: your username, profile photo, bio, music taste, photos and badges (if your profile is private, only friends see the details). Moments you share at a venue are public for 24 hours.
  • Your friends: your online status, last seen, the venue you're at, venues you've visited and events you're going to (you can hide these in Privacy settings).
  • Venues: only total numbers (followers, check-ins, people going, ratings), never who. A venue you send a reservation request to sees the name and phone number you enter, only for that booking. When you use a perk, the venue sees your username for that night.
  • Friends you add to "Safe night": your live location until you end it.
  • If you apply to manage a venue or to become an artist: the Noct team sees what you enter, to verify you.
  • People you block can't see you on the radar or message you.
  • We don't sell your data and we don't show ads.

Where it's stored

Data is stored with Google Firebase (Google Cloud), on servers that may be outside your country.

Deleting your data

Delete your account in Settings → Delete Account. This removes your profile, personal info, photos, moments, plans, reservations, venue and artist requests, your artist profile and Safe night data, and removes you from any venue team. Messages you sent may remain visible to the person you sent them to. Venue totals (like how many people checked in) are not changed.

Your rights

You can ask us what data we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, and object to how we use it, by writing to the address below.
